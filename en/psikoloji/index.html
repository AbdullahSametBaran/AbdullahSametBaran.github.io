Why psychology

For me, psychology is a way of understanding a person not as a single behaviour, but with their past, experiences and circumstances.

What drew me to psychology was a wish to understand people. I wondered why people think, feel and act the way they do, and why the same events meet such different reactions in different people. Over time, that curiosity grew from simply observing behaviour into understanding it within a scientific framework.
